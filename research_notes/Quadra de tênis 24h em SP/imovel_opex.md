## 1. What does it cost per month to rent a 700–3,000 m² terreno or galpão in each candidate region? (listing examples with URLs)

### Takeaway
Across the city, asking rents for 700–3,000 m² galpões mostly fall between **R$ 25 and R$ 35/m²/mês** before IPTU (10 of the 17 priced examples in that size range). Zona Oeste examples run from R$ 21 to R$ 42/m². Logistics-grade galpões with high ceilings near Av. do Estado and Parque da Mooca ask **R$ 45–64/m²**. IPTU adds roughly **R$ 1–6/m²/mês**, and condo galpões also charge condomínio of R$ 5–7/m². Terrenos are cheaper per m², at R$ 15–27/m² in the few examples found (Vila Carrão, plus Mooca where the price comes from the URL only), but they need a structure built on them. Very few galpões listed a pé direito of 10 m or more (Jaguaré 12 m, Parque da Mooca 10 m, Barra Funda 12 m on 4,500 m²). Most listed 5–8 m.

## 2. What is the all-in commercial electricity price per kWh in São Paulo city in 2025–2026? (plus water)

### Takeaway
From 4 Jul 2026, the ANEEL B3 tariff for Enel SP is **R$ 0.78938/kWh before taxes** (TUSD R$ 472.42 + TE R$ 316.96 per MWh, REH 3.596/2026). If the commercial ICMS rate is 18% (assumed, not verified) and PIS/COFINS is about 4–6%, the **all-in price is about R$ 1.01–1.04/kWh** before bandeira and COSIP. Bandeira vermelha patamar 1 adds about R$ 0.057/kWh after taxes. The tarifa branca charges R$ 0.669 (fora ponta), R$ 0.985 (intermediário) and R$ 1.480 (ponta) per kWh before taxes. It only pays off if most lit hours fall outside 16h30–21h30 on weekdays, so it suits the incremental late-night hours but penalizes evening prime time.

|---|---|

## 5. Which Simples Nacional annex applies to CNAE 9311-5/00, and what effective tax rate should be used for revenue of R$ 30k–150k per month?

### Takeaway
CNAE **9311-5/00 (Gestão de instalações de esportes)** is taxed under **Simples Nacional Anexo III**. The **Fator R does not apply**, and the CNAE is **not allowed for MEI**. Accounting sources, not the law text, confirm this. With the unchanged 2026 Anexo III table, the effective rate is **8.60% at R$ 30k/month** (RBT12 R$ 360k), **11.05% at R$ 60k**, **13.03% at R$ 100k** and **14.02% at R$ 150k** (RBT12 R$ 1.8M).

### Inferences
**Effective rates, from my calculation with the table above** (constant monthly revenue, so RBT12 = 12 × monthly revenue):

- For simple models, **10% is a fair single rate for R$ 40–60k/month** and **13–14% for R$ 100–150k/month**.
- Because Fator R does not apply, payroll size does not change the annex.
- The DAS includes ISS, PIS, COFINS, IRPJ, CSLL and, in Anexo III, the CPP (employer INSS). The CPP point is not verified here; see Gaps.

### Gaps
- **Primary legal text not opened.** LC 123/2006 art. 18 and Resolução CGSN 140/2018 (the annex lists) were not checked because pages could not be fetched. The annex classification rests on accounting websites; confirm with the client's accountant.
- **ISS.** Within the Simples, ISS is part of the DAS. Outside the Simples (or above the ISS sublimit), the ISS item for court rental (probably LC 116 item 3.03, which mentions "quadras esportivas") and the São Paulo municipal rate were not verified.
- **First-year RBT12 rule.** For a company in its first 12 months, the proportional RBT12 rule (monthly revenue annualized) changes the band; not verified.
- **CNAE for the lighter model.** The slot-reselling or intermediation model may fit a different CNAE (intermediation or agency) with a different annex. Not researched.
- **CPP inclusion and employer charges.** That CPP is included in the Anexo III DAS, and that FGTS is paid on top, comes from general knowledge and was not verified in this session.
