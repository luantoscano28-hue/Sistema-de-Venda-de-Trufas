## 1. Which zone types allow a sports facility operating late at night, and what are the day vs night noise limits for each zone type?

### Takeaway
Under the LPUOS (Lei 16.402/2016, revised by Lei 18.081/2024), a commercial court facility is a non-residential use that must be allowed for the lot's zone in **Quadro 4**. Sports and leisure services sit in **nR2**, the "tolerável à vizinhança residencial" category (e.g. nR2-10 "serviços de lazer, cultura e esportes" and nR2-11 "local de reunião ou eventos de médio porte", 100–500 people). Only small local sports *associations* (≤100 people, nR1-9) are in the "compatível" nR1 category. I found no zone-specific "night licence". What really limits night operation is **Quadro 4B**, which sets three noise periods (7h–19h / 19h–22h / **22h–7h = night**). At night the limits are about **50 dB(A)** in ZM/ZC/ZEU-type zones, **45 dB(A)** in ZCOR-3 and **40 dB(A)** in the "a" zones, ZCOR-1/ZCORa, ZER and ZPR. PSIU measures this at the complainant's property under NBR 10.151 and enforces it with fines of about R$ 12–15 mil (2025 value R$ 15.401,51), which double on the first repeat, and with **fechamento administrativo** after the third autuação.

### Cited Findings
**Zoning (LPUOS) – use categories**
- The LPUOS splits non-residential use into subcategories. These include **nR1** ("uso não residencial compatível com a vizinhança residencial") and **nR2** ("uso não residencial tolerável à vizinhança residencial") — [Lei 16.402/2016 – Câmara Municipal (PDF)](https://www.saopaulo.sp.leg.br/iah/fulltext/leis/L16402.pdf); [Lei 16.402 versão consolidada – Leis Municipais](https://leismunicipais.com.br/a1/sp/s/sao-paulo/lei-ordinaria/2016/1641/16402/lei-ordinaria-n-16402-2016-disciplina-o-parcelamento-o-uso-e-a-ocupacao-do-solo-no-municipio-de-sao-paulo-de-acordo-com-a-lei-n-16050-de-31-de-julho-de-2014-plano-diretor-estrategico-pde-2016-12-09-versao-consolidada)
- **nR1-9** = "associações comunitárias, culturais e esportivas de caráter local", lotação até 100 pessoas. **nR2-14** covers the same kind of association above the nR1 limit — [Lei 16.402 – Câmara (PDF)](https://www.saopaulo.sp.leg.br/iah/fulltext/leis/L16402.pdf)
- The sports-related nR2 groups are **nR2-10** "serviços de lazer, cultura e esportes", **nR2-11** "local de reunião ou eventos de médio porte" (lotação >100 e até 500 pessoas) and **nR2-14** "associações comunitárias, culturais e esportivas de caráter local" (same range) — [Decreto 57.378/2016 – Câmara (PDF)](https://www.saopaulo.sp.leg.br/iah/fulltext/decretos/D57378.pdf); [Anexo Único do Decreto 57.378/2016](https://drive.prefeitura.sp.gov.br/cidade/secretarias/upload/desenvolvimento_urbano/Decreto_57_378_16_Anexo_Unico.pdf)
- Decreto 57.378/2016 sorts non-residential activities into LPUOS categories, subcategories and groups. Its consolidated annex is "atualizado com resoluções CTLU" (the zoning technical chamber's classification decisions), and **Decreto 63.884/2024** revoked some of its provisions — [Anexo Único (consolidado)](https://drive.prefeitura.sp.gov.br/cidade/secretarias/upload/desenvolvimento_urbano/Decreto_57_378_16_Anexo_Unico.pdf); [Gestão Urbana – Decreto 57.378](https://gestaourbana.prefeitura.sp.gov.br/marco-regulatorio/zoneamento/arquivos/decreto-no-57-378-de-13-de-outubro-de-2016/); [Decreto 57.378 atualizado – Câmara](https://plpconsulta.saopaulo.sp.leg.br/Forms/MostrarArquivo?TIPO=DEC&NUMERO=57378&ANO=2016&DOCUMENTO=Atualizado)
- The annex also has group **nRa-6**, "local para reunião ou eventos de natureza social, esportiva, religiosa, ecoturística, lazer…", capped at 500 m² of built area — [Anexo Único](https://drive.prefeitura.sp.gov.br/cidade/secretarias/upload/desenvolvimento_urbano/Decreto_57_378_16_Anexo_Unico.pdf)
- The zoning law has a separate threshold for "locais destinados à prática de exercício físico ou esporte com mais de 2.500 m² de área construída computável". I could not confirm which article or regime this belongs to — [Decreto 57.378 / LPUOS search reading](https://www.saopaulo.sp.leg.br/iah/fulltext/decretos/D57378.pdf)
- **Lei 18.081/2024** (the LPUOS revision) added § 12 to art. 98, which lets clubs host complementary nR1 activities and authorised events — [Lei 16.402 – Câmara (PDF, consolidated)](https://www.saopaulo.sp.leg.br/iah/fulltext/leis/L16402.pdf)
- **Quadro 4 ("Usos permitidos por zona")** lists for each zone whether each subcategory is allowed (SIM/NÃO). Groups nR1-4 to nR1-12 are SIM in some zones and NÃO in others, while nR1-10 (serviço público social de pequeno porte) is allowed in every zone — [Quadro 4 – anexo da Lei 16.402](https://app-plpconsulta-prd.azurewebsites.net/Forms/MostrarArquivo?TIPO=Lei&NUMERO=16402&ANO=2016&DOCUMENTO=ANEXO&Anexo=4)
- SMUL has an official use-classification service under LPUOS. The law text, maps and quadros are on Gestão Urbana — [SMUL – Usos – Classificação (LPUOS)](https://prefeitura.sp.gov.br/web/licenciamento/w/servicos/265005); [Gestão Urbana – texto da lei, mapas e quadros](https://gestaourbana.prefeitura.sp.gov.br/marco-regulatorio/zoneamento/texto-da-lei/)

**Noise limits (Quadro 4B – "Parâmetros de incomodidade por zona")**
- Art. 113 of the LPUOS requires residential and non-residential uses to meet incomodidade parameters for "I – ruído; II – vibração associada". These are set per zone in Quadro 4B. The noise columns are NCA (nível de critério de avaliação) for the external environment, in dB(A), over three periods: **7h–19h, 19h–22h and 22h–7h** — [Quadro 4B – anexo integrante da Lei 16.402 (Gestão Urbana PDF)](https://gestaourbana.prefeitura.sp.gov.br/wp-content/uploads/2016/03/011-QUADRO_4B_FINAL.pdf)
- Values readable in the **enacted** annex (day / evening / night, dB(A)): **ZM 60/55/50; ZMa 50/45/40; ZCOR-3 55/50/45; ZCORa 50/45/40; ZC 60/55/50 and ZCa 50/45/40** (the ZC/ZCa rows are ambiguous in the text extraction) — [Quadro 4B enacted](https://gestaourbana.prefeitura.sp.gov.br/wp-content/uploads/2016/03/011-QUADRO_4B_FINAL.pdf)
- The **draft** (PL 272/2015) versions of Quadro 4B give: **50/45/40** for the "a" zones (ZEUa, ZCa, ZMa), ZCOR-1, ZER and ZPR; **60/55/50** for ZEU, ZC, ZM and ZMIS; **55/50/45** for ZCOR-3, ZEIS-3 and ZEIS-5; **ZDE-1 60/55/50**; **ZDE-2 and ZPI-1/ZPI-2 65/60/55**. Where they overlap, these match the readable enacted values — [Quadro 4B FINAL (Câmara, PL)](https://saopaulo.sp.leg.br/antigo/wp-content/uploads/dce/zoneamento/QUADRO%204B-FINAL.pdf); [Quadro 4B versão 07/12/15](https://saopaulo.sp.leg.br/antigo/wp-content/uploads/dce/zoneamento/sub/QUADRO_4B_07_12_15_45h.pdf)
- Weekend rule: in the enacted annex, for **atividades de culto only**, on holidays, Saturdays from 14h and Sundays, the 7h–19h limits also apply from 6h–7h and 19h–22h. In the 2015 draft this applied to every activity. It does **not** give sports venues extra leeway — [Quadro 4B enacted](https://gestaourbana.prefeitura.sp.gov.br/wp-content/uploads/2016/03/011-QUADRO_4B_FINAL.pdf); [Quadro 4B draft](https://saopaulo.sp.leg.br/antigo/wp-content/uploads/dce/zoneamento/QUADRO%204B-FINAL.pdf)
- Lei 16.402 (art. 176, per OAB-SP) bans noise above the federal, state or municipal norms, and the most restrictive one prevails — [Cartilha OAB-SP](https://oabsp.org.br/upload/3691383457.pdf)
- Metrô-SP says its works are subject to **ABNT NBR 10.151:2019** and Quadro 4B of Lei 16.402. This is a public body treating both as the operative standard — [ALESP – documento acessório (2021)](https://www.al.sp.gov.br/spl/2021/10/Acessorio/1000390415_1000468973_Acessorio.pdf)
- Subprefeitura Ipiranga calls "before 22h noise is free" a myth: perturbação do sossego is forbidden at any hour, and the dB limits are just much stricter at night — [Subprefeitura Ipiranga](https://prefeitura.sp.gov.br/web/ipiranga/w/pode-ou-n%C3%A3o-pode-no-ipiranga-som-alto-antes-das-22h)
- A consumer guide gives a simplified "50 dB from 7h–22h / 45 dB from 22h–7h in residential zones" — [QuintoAndar](https://www.quintoandar.com.br/guias/manual-imobiliario/lei-do-silencio/). This **conflicts** with Quadro 4B's three periods (ZER 50/45/40 in the draft table). Use Quadro 4B.
- During the zoning revision, a public-consultation proposal asked to redefine the city's permitted noise levels. I don't know the outcome — [Participe+ proposal](https://participemais.prefeitura.sp.gov.br/legislation/processes/159/proposals/2996)

**How PSIU enforcement works**
- Complaints go through **156 / Portal SP156 / Praças de Atendimento** of the Subprefeituras. The complainant must give name, address and phone, which are kept confidential, and should give the establishment's address, the peak-noise hours and the type of activity — [Prefeitura – Psiu](https://prefeitura.sp.gov.br/web/subprefeituras/zeladoria/psiu); [PSIU no combate à poluição sonora](https://prefeitura.sp.gov.br/web/subprefeituras/w/zeladoria/psiu/8831)
- PSIU covers commercial establishments, industries, schools, temples and other non-residential uses, plus construction and sound from parked vehicles. House or condo parties are not covered (police handle those), and public works are excluded — [Prefeitura – Psiu](https://prefeitura.sp.gov.br/web/subprefeituras/zeladoria/psiu); [Sindiconet](https://www.sindiconet.com.br/informese/como-reclamar-de-barulho-de-bares-boates-da-rua-etc-convivencia-barulho-no-condominio)
- Inspections are **scheduled in advance** because they involve Polícia Militar, GCM and sometimes Vigilância Sanitária. Measurement uses the Lei 16.402 limits and the **NBR 10.151** method, **in front of the establishment or at the complainant's home**. If a reading is within limits but the complaint continues, inspectors may measure **inside the complainant's home** (with permission) to find sound leaking through side or back doors and windows — [Prefeitura – Psiu](https://prefeitura.sp.gov.br/web/subprefeituras/zeladoria/psiu); [PSIU no combate](https://prefeitura.sp.gov.br/web/subprefeituras/w/zeladoria/psiu/8831)
- Fines under Lei 16.402 are **R$ 12 mil to R$ 36 mil**. **Art. 148** provides for everything from fines and intimações up to **fechamento administrativo** with police support — [PSIU no combate (Prefeitura)](https://prefeitura.sp.gov.br/web/subprefeituras/w/zeladoria/psiu/8831)
- Folha/Folhapress (May 2026) reports:
  - The fine **doubles at the first repeat offence**. After **three autuações** the establishment gets fechamento administrativo and a fine at triple value.
  - In **Oct 2025** a school was fined **R$ 15.401,51** for **84 dB where the limit was 50 dB**.
  - There was a record of almost **50 mil noise complaints** on 156 in 2025.
  - PSIU went back to fining schools after a court struck down a legal exemption.
  — [Acessa/Folhapress, May 2026](https://www.acessa.com/noticias/2026/05/326759-psiu-volta-a-multar-escolas-em-sp-apos-justica-derrubar-mudanca-em-lei.html)
- **Conflicting or older fine scales**:
  - A Prefeitura news item on "pancadões" describes R$ 10 mil / 20 mil / 30 mil, with closure at the third infraction under Lei 16.402 — [Prefeitura](https://prefeitura.sp.gov.br/w/noticia/prefeitura-amplia-numero-de-fiscais-do-psiu-e-201cpancadoes201d)
  - A noise consultancy cites R$ 8 mil–30 mil — [GNR Ambiental](https://gnrambiental.com.br/noticias/multas-do-psiu-valores-e-como-a-gnr-ambiental-pode-ajudar-a-anula-las/)
  - An old Jusbrasil article (pre-2016 regime) cites R$ 10.701 / R$ 18 mil for exceeding the limit by 30 dB — [Jusbrasil](https://carollinasalle.jusbrasil.com.br/noticias/177516982/lei-do-silencio-como-lidar-com-vizinhos-barulhentos)

The secretary said the technical dB limits do **not** change and the text keeps the PDE/LPUOS indices — [Rádio Bandeirantes, 21/05/2026](https://www.band.com.br/radio-bandeirantes/noticias/prefeitura-de-sp-propoe-lei-para-endurecer-fiscalizacao-do-psiu-202605211402)

**Time-of-day rules that do exist**
- **Lei 12.879/1999 ("Lei da 1 hora")**: bars may not operate between **1h and 5h** if they work with open doors, without acoustic insulation, without parking and without security staff, or if they disturb public peace. Bars in hotels, flats, **clubs, associations** and hospitals are excluded — [Lei 12.879/1999 – Leis Municipais](https://leismunicipais.com.br/a1/sp/s/sao-paulo/lei-ordinaria/1999/1287/12879/lei-ordinaria-n-12879-1999-dispoe-sobre-o-horario-de-funcionamento-dos-bares-na-cidade-de-sao-paulo); [Jus.com.br](https://jus.com.br/artigos/1915/a-lei-de-fechamento-de-bares-em-sao-paulo)
- To stay open after 1h a bar needs a **specific licence requiring acoustics, security and its own parking** — [Prefeitura – Conheça as leis contra o barulho](https://prefeitura.sp.gov.br/web/comunicacao/w/noticias/129453). The soundproofing must be certified by an engineer hired by the owner, parking may be valet, and security must be provided by registered professionals — [Prefeitura – fiscalização da Lei da 1 hora](https://prefeitura.sp.gov.br/web/comunicacao/w/noticias/121939)
- In 2013 the MP criticised the overlap of rules for letting bars operate from 5h to 1h with loud sound after 22h — [Conjur, 01/07/2013](https://www.conjur.com.br/2013-jul-01/confusao-leis-sao-paulo-permite-som-alto-horario-noturno-critica-mp/)

### Inferences
- **No zone "allows" 24h operation as such.** Night operation is legal only if the noise received at neighbours stays under the zone's 22h–7h limit (and, if alcohol is served on-site, if the Lei da 1 hora is met). The zones with the most headroom are **ZEU/ZEM/ZC/ZM (50 dB(A) at night)**, then ZCOR-3 (45). ZDE/ZPI have 55 dB(A) at night in the draft table, but whether they allow nR2 leisure uses is unconfirmed. "a" zones, ZCOR-1, ZER and ZPR (40 dB(A) at night) are very hostile, and as exclusively residential zones ZER/ZPR most likely **do not permit** a commercial nR2 use at all. Confirm this in Quadro 4.
- A commercial court-rental company most likely falls in **nR2-10 ("serviços de lazer, cultura e esportes")**, or nR2-11 if treated as a local de reunião. It would not fall in nR1-9, which is for associations of local character. Being nR2 ("tolerável") rather than nR1 ("compatível") signals the city sees it as a potential nuisance, so expect it to be barred or restricted in purely residential and corridor zones. SMUL/CTLU must confirm the exact group.
- **Pinheiros / Zona Oeste:** mixed and dense, with ZEU/ZM/ZC along the Faria Lima, Rebouças and Line-4 axes and ZER/ZCOR pockets nearby. A lot in ZM/ZEU can still sit next to residential towers, and **PSIU measures where the complainant lives**. Outdoor tennis between 22h and 7h (impulsive ball impacts, voices, and the sound of floodlights and HVAC) is very likely to break 40–50 dB(A) at a neighbour's window. Realistically, night operation needs fully enclosed courts with acoustic treatment and a pre-lease acoustic study (laudo acústico per NBR 10.151) by an acoustic engineer.
- **Enforcement escalates fast:** about R$ 15 mil on the first autuação (2025 value), about R$ 30 mil on the second, and closure on the third. A single determined neighbour using 156 could close the business within months. The May 2026 bill to strengthen night inspection points to tighter, not looser, enforcement.
- The Lei da 1 hora matters only if the venue runs a **bar / on-site alcohol consumption**. The club and association exclusion probably doesn't cover a commercial court-rental company (lawyer to confirm).

## 2. Which licenses and permits are needed, and what are their typical timelines and costs?

### Takeaway
There are two parallel tracks:
- **Municipal:** an **Auto de Licença de Funcionamento (ALF)** through Empreenda Fácil / VRE Redesim, which is instant and online if the activity is low-risk. If capacity (lotação) is **250 or more people**, it becomes an **Alvará de Funcionamento de Local de Reunião** issued by SMUL.
- **State fire safety:** an **AVCB or CLCB** from the Corpo de Bombeiros (CBPMESP) under **Decreto Estadual 69.118/2024** and the **2025 ITs**. Sports centres fall in division **F-3**. A CLCB lasts **2 years** for F-3, and the Projeto Técnico Simplificado applies to group F up to **250 people**, with panic bars required above **100**.

I found no official fee or processing-time figures beyond "immediate for low-risk ALF" (secondary sources); a despachante must quote them.

### Cited Findings
**Municipal operating licence**
- Establishments with **capacity of 250 people or more** that run public-generating activities need an **Alvará de Funcionamento para local de reunião**, issued by **SMUL**. All other cases use the **ALF**. At the Subprefeitura, the custas processuais are calculated after the process is filed (official page about 2 years old) — [Prefeitura – ALF](https://prefeitura.sp.gov.br/web/subprefeituras/w/deguos/344410)
- The ALF is issued through **Empreenda Fácil inside VRE Redesim**. For **low-risk** activities it is issued electronically and immediately, without prior inspection — [CompanyHero](https://www.companyhero.com/blog/como-emitir-alvara-de-funcionamento-prefeitura-de-sao-paulo); [Rei do Alvará – ALF](https://www.reidoalvara.com.br/auto-de-licenca-de-funcionamento-alf-sao-paulo)
- Licensing firms quote low-risk timelines of "a few days", "24 hours" and "a few hours" — [Licenciei – Alvará em 24 horas](https://licenciei.com.br/alvara-de-funcionamento/); [PL Brasil](https://plbrasil.com.br/alvara-funcionamento-sao-paulo/); [CompanyHero](https://www.companyhero.com/blog/como-emitir-alvara-de-funcionamento-prefeitura-de-sao-paulo). These are secondary, commercial sources.
- One licensing-firm article says that to count as low risk the activity must be on the municipal list **and** the premises must be in a building of up to **1.500 m²** total area (or a location of up to 500 m²) — [PL Brasil](https://plbrasil.com.br/alvara-funcionamento-sao-paulo/) (secondary; confirm)

**Fire safety (Corpo de Bombeiros – CBPMESP)**
- **Decreto nº 69.118, de 9/12/2024** created the current Regulamento de Segurança contra Incêndio for the state of São Paulo. The **CLCB** is issued after documents are submitted proving the building meets fire-safety requirements; the **AVCB** is the inspection-based certificate — [Decreto 69.118/2024 – ALESP](https://www.al.sp.gov.br/repositorio/legislacao/decreto/2024/decreto-69118-09.12.2024.html); [Guia SegCI – Decreto 69.118](https://guiasegci.com.br/legislacoes/decreto-69-118-regulamento-de-seguranca-contra-incendio/)
- The decree's **Tabela 6F-2** covers divisions **F-3**, F-4 and F-9 with **area over 750 m² or height over 12 m**, and points to **IT-12** for F-3 — [Decreto 69.118 – DOE (PDF)](https://guiasegci.com.br/wp-content/uploads/2024/12/Decreto-69.118-de-9-de-dezembro-de-2024-1.pdf)
- A consultancy blog says the CLCB is the simplified route for low-risk buildings **up to 750 m²**, with lotação of about 100 people and no complex systems. I did not see this criterion in the decree text — [DRD2 (secondary)](https://www.drd2.com.br/blog/como-emitir-clcb-em-sao-paulo-passo-a-passo)
- **IT 01/2025** (procedimentos administrativos):
  - In force since Feb 2025, replacing IT 01/2019 (Portaria CCB-002/800/25), and amended by **Portaria CCB-004/800/2025 of 19/03/2025**, which changed AVCB/CLCB validity periods.
  - **CLCB validity:** 3 years for F-1/F-2, 1 year for football stadiums, **2 years for other F-3 uses**.
  - Group F projects must state the **lotação of each room**.
  — [Guia SegCI – IT 01/2025](https://guiasegci.com.br/legislacoes/it-01-2025-procedimentos-administrativos/)
- **IT 42/2025 (Projeto Técnico Simplificado – PTS)** sets a maximum lotação of **250 people for group F**, and requires **barra antipânico** for group F above **100 people** (per IT 11) — [Guia SegCI – IT 42/2025](https://guiasegci.com.br/legislacoes/it-42-2025-projeto-tecnico-simplificado-pts/)
- **IT 17/2025 (brigada de incêndio)** sizes the number of brigadistas by **lotação máxima** — [IT 17/2025 (PDF)](https://www.fef.unicamp.br/wp-content/uploads/sites/70/2026/02/tormqmwsarfmcnxpu6cn_it-n-17-25-brigada-de-incndio.pdf). **IT 18/2025** (iluminação de emergência) uses a 100-person threshold for some balizamento requirements — [Guia SegCI – IT 18/2025](https://guiasegci.com.br/legislacoes/it-18-2025-iluminacao-de-emergencia/)
- **IT 43/2025** covers adapting **existing buildings** to the fire-safety rules, which matters when retrofitting a leased warehouse — [IT 43/2025 (PDF)](https://guiasegci.com.br/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/IT-43-25-Adaptacao-as-normas-de-seguranca-contra-incendio-edificacoes-existentes.pdf); dozens of ITs were updated in 2025 — [SindusCon-SP](https://sindusconsp.com.br/bombeiros-atualizam-dezenas-de-instrucoes-tecnicas/)
- **Enforcement example:** MPSP obtained an injunction stopping a beach-sports venue in the Zona Sul (Praia Hall) from opening to the public **until it had an alvará and AVCB** (details in Q3) — [MPSP](https://www.mpsp.mp.br/w/espa%C3%A7o-de-beach-sports-na-zona-sul-de-s%C3%A3o-paulo-%C3%A9-interditado-por-falta-de-alvar%C3%A1-e-avcb)

### Inferences
- **Capacity design drives the licensing path.** Keeping the declared lotação **under 250** keeps the venue on the ALF route rather than the Local de Reunião alvará. Staying **at or under 100** also avoids panic-bar and other F-group requirements and matches nR1/nR2 capacity bands. Spectator stands or bleachers can push lotação up quickly.
- **Area:** this is general knowledge, not sourced here. A standard tennis court with run-off is about 36.6 × 18.3 m ≈ **670 m²**, so a single covered court sits near the **750 m²** CLCB/Tabela 6F-2 line, and **two or more courts will exceed it** and probably need a full AVCB process (Projeto Técnico, inspection, IT-12 for F-3). Covered courts also count toward the **2.500 m²** "prática de exercício físico ou esporte" threshold noted in Q1 (likely a traffic-impact / Polo Gerador de Tráfego trigger; confirm).
- "Low-risk, instant ALF" probably **won't** apply cleanly to a public-facing sports venue with night operation and possibly a bar. Plan for a manual analysis path and keep the AVCB/CLCB and building-regularity papers ready.
- **Pre-lease checklist** (for the architect, despachante and lawyer):
  1. GeoSampa zone of the lot, plus Quadro 4 permission for the relevant nR2 group.
  2. The Quadro 4B night limit for that zone, and an acoustic study at the nearest residential receivers.
  3. Building regularity (Habite-se / Auto de Regularização) and fire-code feasibility (F-3, area, lotação, IT 43 for existing buildings).
  4. Lotação design: under 250, ideally at or under 100.
  5. Any history of PSIU or 156 complaints at the address, and neighbour mapping.
  6. A lease clause making the lease conditional on getting the ALF and AVCB/CLCB (condição suspensiva) and allowing exit if PSIU stops night use.

### Gaps
- **No official fee or timeline** was found for the ALF, the Local de Reunião alvará, or the AVCB/CLCB (Bombeiros taxa, project and inspection lead times). The annual **TFE (Taxa de Fiscalização de Estabelecimentos)** was not checked. A despachante must quote these.
- Whether "aluguel de quadras / atividades esportivas" is on São Paulo's **low-risk list** (dispensa or immediate licence under the Lei de Liberdade Econômica) is unverified.
- **Accessibility was not researched** because the search budget was exhausted. Points to verify with an architect: Lei Federal 13.146/2015 (LBI), whose art. 60 §1º is generally understood to tie the granting or renewal of an alvará de funcionamento to compliance with accessibility rules; **ABNT NBR 9050:2020**; and the São Paulo Código de Obras (Lei 16.642/2017, Decreto 57.776/2017). All unverified in this session.
- Whether the ALF requires an Auto de Conclusão/Habite-se or a regularised building, and how CLCB eligibility interacts with F-3 and lotação in IT 01/2025, both need official confirmation.

## 4. Is an AED legally required at sports facilities in São Paulo?

### Takeaway
Only above thresholds. Municipal **Lei 13.945/2005**, amended by **Lei 14.621/2007** and **Lei 15.283/2010** and regulated by **Decreto 49.277/2008**, requires an AED in places such as **clubs and gyms with more than 1.000 sócios** and places with **concentration above 1.000 people or average daily circulation above 3.000**. A small court-rental venue probably falls **below** these thresholds, so an AED is likely **not legally mandatory**, though it is strongly advisable for a 24h sports venue. Where the law does apply, trained staff must be present during all operating hours, which conflicts with unstaffed operation.

### Cited Findings
- **Lei 13.945, de 7/1/2005** (municipal, city of São Paulo) made it mandatory to keep an automatic external defibrillator in places with a concentration or average daily circulation of **1.500 or more people**, listing among others clubs, gyms and workplaces. Art. 4 provided for regulation within 90 days — [Lei 13.945/2005 – Câmara (PDF)](https://www.saopaulo.sp.leg.br/iah/fulltext/leis/L13945.pdf); [Catálogo de Legislação Municipal](https://legislacao.prefeitura.sp.gov.br/leis/lei-13945-de-07-de-janeiro-de-2005)
- **Lei 14.621, de 11/12/2007** (from the Executive's PL 18/2006) rewrote art. 1º. It now covers "**os clubes e academias com mais de 1.000 (mil) sócios**", places with **concentration above 1.000 people** or **average daily circulation above 3.000 people**, and financial and educational institutions at about **1.500 people/day** — [Lei 14.621/2007 – Câmara (PDF)](https://www.saopaulo.sp.leg.br/iah/fulltext/leis/L14621.pdf); [PL 18/2006](https://www.saopaulo.sp.leg.br/iah/fulltext/projeto/PL0018-2006.pdf); [Prefeitura – "locais com mais de mil pessoas precisam de desfibriladores"](https://prefeitura.sp.gov.br/web/comunicacao/w/noticias/133334)
- The Prefeitura cites "Leis 13.945/05 e suas alterações (leis 14.621/07 e 15.283/10), regulamentada pelo **Decreto 49.277/08**". The device must be registered with **ANVISA** — [Prefeitura/Covisa](https://prefeitura.sp.gov.br/web/saude/w/noticias/231508)
- **Decreto 49.277, de 4/3/2008** requires that:
  - The process lets the AED reach the patient **within 5 minutes** of the event.
  - Up-to-date records of **trained staff** are kept, to prove their **presence throughout the operating period**.
  - The CIPA, the fire brigade and **two more employees per shift per device** are trained.
  - Establishments with their own medical service keep a médico responsável técnico present.
  - Equipment follows ANVISA, ABNT and INMETRO norms.
  - Enforcement is by the Secretaria Municipal da Saúde.
  — [Decreto 49.277/2008 – Câmara (PDF)](https://www.saopaulo.sp.leg.br/iah/fulltext/decretos/D49277.pdf); [Decreto 49.277/2008 – Leis Municipais](https://leismunicipais.com.br/a/sp/s/sao-paulo/decreto/2008/4928/49277/decreto-n-49277-2008-regulamenta-a-lei-n-13945-de-7-de-janeiro-de-2005-alterada-pela-lei-n-14621-de-11-de-dezembro-de-2007-que-dispoe-sobre-a-obrigatoriedade-da-manutencao-de-aparelho-desfibrilador-externo-automatico-nos-locais-que-designa-revoga-o-decreto-n-46914-de-17-de-janeiro-de-1996)
- **Penalty example:** Covisa fined Shopping Cidade Jardim over the AED and posto médico law. The fine was **R$ 3.779,64, applied weekly** until the establishment complied (date not captured) — [Prefeitura/Covisa](https://prefeitura.sp.gov.br/web/saude/w/noticias/231508)
- A Coren-SP guidance note on AED use mentions a constitutionality challenge to the law that ended in **arquivamento** (no incompatibility found) — [Coren-SP](https://portal.coren-sp.gov.br/sites/default/files/Orienta%C3%A7%C3%A3o%20Fundamentada%20-%20035_2.pdf)
- One blog presents the AED requirement as a **state** law; the searches indicate Lei 13.945/2005 is **municipal**, so the blog is unreliable on this point — [Toth Lifecare blog](https://www.tothlifecare.com.br/blog/obrigatoriedade-do-desfibrilador-externo-automatico-no-estado-de-sao-paulo)

### Inferences
- A 1–4 court rental venue (dozens to low hundreds of users a day) almost certainly stays under the **1.000 concurrent / 3.000 daily** thresholds. It would only fall under "clubes e academias" if structured as a club or gym with **more than 1.000 sócios**. A large subscription or "membership" app model could arguably be read as sócios, which a lawyer should assess.
- If the threshold is reached, the decree's demand for **trained people present during all operating hours** effectively rules out a fully unstaffed 24h model.
- Even if not mandatory, an AED is a cheap mitigation of liability for a venue with **unsupervised late-night exercise**. Civil liability under consumer law for failing to give basic emergency assistance is a plausible risk; lawyer to confirm.

### Gaps
- I couldn't retrieve the **consolidated text of art. 1º after Lei 15.283/2010**, so its changes to the thresholds are unknown.
- I didn't check whether a **state** (ALESP) or **federal** rule, such as the Lei Geral do Esporte (Lei 14.597/2023), now requires AEDs at sports venues of this size. The search budget was exhausted.

## 5. Are there rules affecting unstaffed operation, minors at night, or alcohol sales late at night?

### Takeaway
I found **no rule that expressly bans an unstaffed (self-service) sports venue** in São Paulo. However, several obligations assume people on site:
- the **Lei da 1 hora** (security staff, acoustics and parking for selling alcohol on-site between 1h and 5h);
- the AED decree (trained staff present, if it applies);
- the fire brigade sized by lotação (IT 17/2025).

For **minors**, ECA art. 149 lets the Vara da Infância e da Juventude regulate by portaria when unaccompanied children and teenagers may enter sports venues. STJ case law bars generic portarias, and I found **no current São Paulo capital portaria**. Selling or serving **alcohol** late at night without staff (e.g. self-service fridges) is high-risk; age verification and the state law on sales to minors still need to be confirmed.

### Cited Findings
- **Alcohol, 1h–5h:** under Lei 12.879/1999 a bar may operate from 1h to 5h only with acoustic insulation (certified by an engineer), its own parking (valet allowed) and **security staff from registered professionals**. Otherwise it must close from 1h to 5h. Hotel bars, flats, clubs, associations and hospitals are excluded — [Lei 12.879/1999](https://leismunicipais.com.br/a1/sp/s/sao-paulo/lei-ordinaria/1999/1287/12879/lei-ordinaria-n-12879-1999-dispoe-sobre-o-horario-de-funcionamento-dos-bares-na-cidade-de-sao-paulo); [Prefeitura – leis contra o barulho](https://prefeitura.sp.gov.br/web/comunicacao/w/noticias/129453); [Prefeitura – Lei da 1 hora](https://prefeitura.sp.gov.br/web/comunicacao/w/noticias/121939)
- A noise-measurement blog cites an initial fine of **R$ 24.195,00** with possible closure in the São Paulo noise and bar context. It is unverified and should not be relied on — [Ruído Ambiental (blog)](https://www.ruido-ambiental.com/post/medicao-ruido-sao-paulo)
- **Minors:** **ECA art. 149** gives the judge power to regulate, by portaria or alvará, the entry and stay of **unaccompanied children and teenagers** in places such as stadiums, sports facilities, dances, nightclubs, electronic-games venues and studios — [ECA art. 149 – Modelo Inicial](https://modeloinicial.com.br/lei/ECA/estatuto-crianca-do-adolescente/art-149); [Jusbrasil – art. 149](https://www.jusbrasil.com.br/topicos/10595518/artigo-149-da-lei-n-8069-de-13-de-julho-de-1990)
- **STJ:** portarias under art. 149 must be grounded case by case. **General, abstract portarias are forbidden**, and one that set general rules was annulled — [Buscador Dizer o Direito](https://www.buscadordizerodireito.com.br/jurisprudencia/detalhes/e60e81c4cbe5171cd654662d9887aec2); [MPRJ – estudo sobre portarias do art. 149](https://www.mprj.mp.br/documents/20184/2742148/Rosa_Carneiro.pdf)
- For comparison, another state's capital (**Belém/PA**, Portaria 002/2026 of the 1ª VIJ) bans teenagers from bars and raves even when accompanied, and sets a **20h limit** for 12–16-year-olds at dances and nightclubs. This does **not** apply in São Paulo; it only shows how such portarias look — [TJPA Portaria 002/2026](https://www.tjpa.jus.br/CMSPortal/VisualizarArquivo?idArquivo=2269939)
- MPSP has acted against events held **without an alvará from both the Prefeitura and the Vara da Infância e Juventude** (2014 item; only the title was seen) — [MPSP 2014](http://www.mpsp.mp.br/portal/page/portal/noticias/publicacao_noticias/2014/2014_outubro/Evento%20n%C3%A3o%20tinha%20alvar%C3%A1%20da%20Prefeitura%20nem%20da%20Vara%20da%20Inf%C3%A2ncia%20e%20Juventude)
- **Staff-dependent duties:**
  - If the AED law applies, trained staff must be present throughout operating hours — [Decreto 49.277/2008](https://www.saopaulo.sp.leg.br/iah/fulltext/decretos/D49277.pdf)
  - IT 17/2025 sizes the **brigada de incêndio** by lotação — [IT 17/2025](https://www.fef.unicamp.br/wp-content/uploads/sites/70/2026/02/tormqmwsarfmcnxpu6cn_it-n-17-25-brigada-de-incndio.pdf)
  - PSIU inspections can happen at night with PM and GCM, and the operator must deal with them — [Prefeitura – Psiu](https://prefeitura.sp.gov.br/web/subprefeituras/zeladoria/psiu)

### Inferences
- **Unstaffed operation is not expressly forbidden**, but it works only if the venue:
  - (a) is **below the AED thresholds**;
  - (b) has a fire-safety classification whose lotação doesn't require a permanent brigade (confirm with the fire-safety engineer under IT 17/2025);
  - (c) **does not sell alcohol for on-site consumption between 1h and 5h** (Lei da 1 hora requires security staff);
  - (d) has a remote-monitoring and emergency-response plan.
- **Alcohol:** the safest 24h setup is **no alcohol sales at night**, especially with no staff. A self-service fridge can't reliably verify age, and selling to under-18s is illegal in Brazil (see Gaps). Even with staff, an on-site bar open after 1h triggers the Lei da 1 hora (acoustics, parking, security). A commercial court company probably can't use the club or association exclusion (lawyer to confirm).
- **Minors at night:** no São Paulo capital rule found bans teenagers from playing tennis at night. Because ECA art. 149 lets a judge regulate sports venues, and because of liability for unsupervised minors, a house rule is prudent: under-18s only with a guardian or written authorisation after a set hour (e.g. 22h), with ID checks built into the booking app. A lawyer should confirm.

### Gaps
- **The state law on alcohol sales to minors (commonly cited as Lei Estadual 14.592/2011) and ECA art. 243** were **not verified** in this session because the search budget ran out. Their penalties (fines, interdiction) and how they apply to **self-service or vending alcohol** need a lawyer's confirmation.
- I found no specific municipal or state rule on **"autoatendimento" / unstaffed venues** (e.g. the rules on mercados autônomos in condos) and couldn't run the search.
- I found no **current portaria of the Vara da Infância e Juventude da Capital (TJSP)** on minors in sports venues at night. Check TJSP and the DJe.
- Whether a **snack bar or cantina inside a sports venue** counts as a "bar" under Lei 12.879/1999 is untested; lawyer to confirm.
